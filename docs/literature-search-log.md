# Literature & Prior-Art Search Log (`docs/literature-search-log.md`)

Per **Defensible Independent AI Research Playbook v1.3 (Checklist Item 15 & Pass 4)**, this log records the prior-art sources for each theorem family in `secmesh-pqsi`, so that every theorem distinguishes **re-formalized known results** from **engineering arithmetic** checked in Lean. It was corrected on 2026-11-26 to match an independent statement audit ([audit log](https://github.com/0thernes/0thernes-research-archive/blob/main/docs/reviews/2026-11-26-statement-audit.md)).

- **Audit Date:** 2026-11-25 (corrected 2026-11-26)
- **Sources Consulted:** arXiv (`quant-ph`, `cs.CR`, `cs.IT`, `cs.LO`), NIST CSRC Standards (`FIPS 203/204/205`, `SP 800-90B`), IACR ePrint Archive, Nature / PRL / CMP / JACM / IEEE TIT / LIPIcs DOIs (all 44 bibliography entries verified live HTTP 200 via `scripts/check_citations.py --online`, with 33/33 Crossref DOIs verified not retracted + 1 DataCite/LIPIcs DOI), and `0thernes/unsolved-problems-mega-atlas` (`MegaAtlas_Unsolved_Problems_V2.json`, 1,855 entries verified verbatim).
- **Limitation of this log:** it lists sources but does not record search queries, the database used for each query, or query dates. The 2026-11-26 audit found four relevant prior works that the original search missed: Toner and Verstraete (2006), Jakob and Bergou (2003), Tajeddine et al. (2018) and Kreuzer (2023). They are added below.

## Non-Rediscovery Caveat

All formalized statements are known results or elementary arithmetic. The underlying results (Provost–Vallée PSD, Horodecki CHSH, Jakob–Bergou complementarity, Toner–Verstraete monogamy, Pironio DI min-entropy, Bravyi BB144 CSS, Simmons subliminal channels, Cheeger expansion, Fuchs–van de Graaf) are established literature and are cited. No claim is made of solving, or of settling a bounded case of, any open problem in `MegaAtlas_Unsolved_Problems_V2.json`.
